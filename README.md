# Research Projects in Sustainable Marketing and Digital Innovation

This repository contains three independent research papers completed during my MSc in Marketing at the University of Nottingham Ningbo (2025–2026). These projects reflect my early academic explorations in sustainable marketing, digital strategy, and platform-based innovation.

They collectively demonstrate my ability to:
- Synthesize literature and identify research gaps
- Evaluate real-world business cases using structured frameworks
- Formulate strategic recommendations grounded in theory and analysis

These skills and research interests have now converged toward my PhD research focus: **how marketing resource allocation in energy firms affects low‑carbon investment (energy storage & renewables), and the moderating role of capital market valuation feedback**.

---

## 📄 Projects

### 1. Barriers to Circular Economy and Sustainable Product Development
- **Type** : Systematic literature review  
- **Summary** : Identified key obstacles (consumer, operational, business model) hindering the transition from linear to circular economies. Proposed an integrated conceptual framework to guide future empirical research.

### 2. Digital Presence Evaluation and Strategic Improvement for Aesop
- **Type** : Case study + strategic plan  
- **Summary** : Conducted a digital audit of Aesop’s online presence (website, social media, e‑commerce). Evaluated brand positioning and engagement metrics. Developed a 4‑point improvement plan covering UX, personalization, and omnichannel integration.

### 3. Co‑creation and Open Innovation on Taobao Platform
- **Type** : Case study  
- **Summary** : Analyzed how Taobao’s platform design and feedback mechanisms enable co‑creation between sellers and consumers. Identified key enablers (crowdsourcing, iterative prototyping, community governance) and discussed implications for platform‑mediated innovation.

## 🔗 Connection to My PhD Research

Although these projects are not directly on energy or finance, they built my foundational competence in:
- Framing research questions
- Selecting appropriate methodologies (literature review, case analysis, strategic evaluation)
- Communicating findings clearly

My current PhD‑level research (master’s thesis) extends these skills to an **empirical panel data study** of Chinese A‑share energy firms (2018–2025, CSMAR database), applying **two‑way fixed effects models** to test hypotheses on marketing investment, low‑carbon investment, and ownership heterogeneity.
